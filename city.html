My World

06. My City

A1 Reading & Listening
🎧 LISTEN TO THE TEXT

Listen carefully. You can follow the text below.

I live in a beautiful city. It is not very small, and there are many interesting places to visit.

There are many shops, cafés, parks, and restaurants in my city. I like going to the park because I can walk, relax, and enjoy the fresh air.

There are also many tall buildings and busy streets. In the city centre, there are many people, cars, and buses.

My favourite place is a small café near my home. I like meeting my friends there and drinking coffee together.

I love my city because it is beautiful, comfortable, and full of life.
