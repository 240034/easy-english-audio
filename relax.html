07. Time to Relax

A1 Reading & Listening
🎧 LISTEN TO THE TEXT

Listen carefully. You can follow the text below.

Everyone needs some time to relax after a busy day. Taking a short break can help us feel better and have more energy.

When I want to relax, I usually go to a quiet place. Sometimes I sit on the sofa, listen to music, or read a book.

I also enjoy watching a movie or talking to my friends. These simple activities help me forget about my work and everyday problems.

When the weather is nice, I prefer going outside. A short walk in the park can help me clear my mind.

It is important not to be busy all the time. Our bodies and minds need rest, even when we have many things to do.

I think everyone should find their own way to relax. A little quiet time every day can make a big difference.
