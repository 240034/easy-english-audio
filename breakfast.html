03. Breakfast Around World

A1 Reading & Listening
🎧 LISTEN TO THE TEXT

Listen carefully. You can follow the text below.

Breakfast is an important meal around the world. People in different countries eat different food in the morning.

In Japan, people often eat rice, fish, and soup. They may also have vegetables for breakfast.

In France, many people like bread and croissants. They often drink coffee, tea, or hot chocolate.

In the United States, a popular breakfast can include eggs, toast, and fruit. Some people also eat pancakes with syrup.

In Kazakhstan, people may eat eggs, bread, porridge, or traditional food. Families often have breakfast together at home.

Breakfast is different in every country. It is interesting to see what people eat in the morning around the world.
