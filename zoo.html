05. Animals at the Zoo

A1 Reading & Listening
🎧 LISTEN TO THE TEXT

Listen carefully. You can follow the text below.

Visiting a zoo gives people a chance to discover many wild animals up close.

Monkeys are active and curious, often jumping between tree branches and watching visitors.

Lions, giraffes, and elephants live in large open enclosures designed to mimic their natural habitats.

A visit to the zoo can be an educational experience for both children and adults.

Visitors must show respect to the animals by keeping quiet and avoiding feeding them without permission.

Modern zoos focus on animal conservation and teaching people how to protect wildlife.
