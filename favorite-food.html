01. My Favourite Food

A1 Reading & Listening
🎧 LISTEN TO THE TEXT

Listen carefully. You can follow the text below.

My favorite food is pizza. I like pizza because it is delicious and easy to eat.

There are many kinds of pizza. My favorite one has cheese, tomatoes, chicken, and mushrooms.

I usually eat pizza with my family or friends. We often order it at home on Friday evening.

Sometimes we make pizza at home. We put the ingredients on the pizza and cook it in the oven.

I like trying new kinds of pizza. Sometimes I choose a new topping, but cheese is always my favorite.

I don't eat pizza every day, but it is always a good choice when I want something tasty. Pizza is one of my favorite foods!

📚 KEY VOCABULARY
delicious вкусный
kind вид, разновидность
mushroom гриб
ingredient ингредиент
oven духовка
topping начинка, топпинг
choose выбирать
tasty вкусный
