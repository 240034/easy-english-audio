My World

02. My Best Friend

A1 Reading & Listening
🎧 LISTEN TO THE TEXT

Listen carefully. You can follow the text below.

I have a best friend. Her name is Anna. She is very kind, friendly, and funny. We study together and spend a lot of time with each other.

Anna has long brown hair and beautiful brown eyes. She is tall and slim. She likes wearing comfortable clothes, such as jeans and T-shirts.

My best friend has many hobbies. She likes reading books, listening to music, and watching movies. Her favourite hobby is painting. She can paint beautiful pictures.

We often meet after school. We go for walks, talk about our day, and drink coffee together. At the weekend, we sometimes go to the cinema or visit new places.

I am very happy to have such a wonderful friend. She always makes me smile!

📚 KEY VOCABULARY
meet встретиться
slim стройный
comfortable удобный
hobby хобби, увлечение
