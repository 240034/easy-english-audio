07. Healthy Food

A1 Reading & Listening
🎧 LISTEN TO THE TEXT

Listen carefully. You can follow the text below.

Healthy food helps us feel good and have energy. We need different kinds of food every day.

Fruit and vegetables are very healthy. They have many vitamins and can be part of every meal.

Fish, eggs, and meat are also important foods. They give our bodies protein and help us stay strong.

It is important to drink enough water every day. Water is good for our body and helps us feel fresh.

We can also eat sweets and fast food sometimes. However, it is better not to eat them every day.

Healthy food does not have to be boring. There are many simple and delicious healthy meals.
