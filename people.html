My World

09. People Around The World

A1 Reading & Listening
🎧 LISTEN TO THE TEXT

Listen carefully. You can follow the text below.

People live in many different countries around the world. They speak different languages, eat different food, and have different traditions.

In Japan, people often eat rice, fish, and vegetables. Many people also enjoy tea.

In Italy, people love pizza and pasta. Families often spend time together and enjoy long meals.

In Brazil, people love music, dancing, and football. Brazil is also famous for its beautiful beaches.

In Kazakhstan, people enjoy traditional food, family celebrations, and spending time with guests.

Every country is different, but people everywhere like spending time with family and friends.
