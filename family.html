My World

01. My Family

A1 Reading & Listening
🎧 LISTEN TO THE TEXT

Listen carefully. You can follow the text below.

Family is very important to me. I have a small and happy family. There are four people in my family: my mother, my father, my brother, and me.

My mother is kind and friendly. She likes cooking and watching TV. My father is funny and hardworking. He likes reading books and going for walks.

I have one younger brother. We like playing games and watching movies together. Sometimes we play outside or go to the park.

We also have a dog. His name is Max. He is very friendly and playful. He likes running, playing with a ball, and sleeping on the sofa. We love spending time with him.

We live in a nice house. In the evening, we usually have dinner together and talk about our day. At the weekend, we sometimes watch a movie or go for a walk.

I love my family very much. We help each other, spend time together, and have a lot of fun!
