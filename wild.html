04. Wild Animals

A1 Reading & Listening
🎧 LISTEN TO THE TEXT

Listen carefully. You can follow the text below.

Unlike pets, wild animals live in nature without human help. They inhabit forests, mountains, deserts, and oceans.

Wild animals must find their own food and water to survive in their natural environments.

They also need enough space to hunt, build shelters, and raise their young safety.

Unfortunately, human activity is causing significant threats to their natural habitats.

Problems like deforestation and pollution destroy the homes of many species every year.

Protecting wild animals and their environments is vital to maintaining balance in nature.
