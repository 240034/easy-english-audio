01. My Favourite Hobby

A1 Reading & Listening
🎧 LISTEN TO THE TEXT

Listen carefully. You can follow the text below.

Everyone has something they enjoy doing in their free time. My favorite hobby is reading because it helps me relax and learn new things.

I usually read in the evening when I finish my work or studies. I like sitting in a quiet place with a cup of tea.

I enjoy different kinds of books, but I especially like stories and fantasy. Interesting characters make me want to read more.

Reading also helps me improve my English. When I find a new word, I usually write it down and check its meaning.

Sometimes I talk about books with my friends. We recommend books to each other and share our opinions.

I think a good hobby should be both enjoyable and useful. Reading gives me both, so I hope I will always have time for it.

hobby хобби, увлечение
